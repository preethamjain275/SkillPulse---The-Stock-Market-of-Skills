# PROBLEM STATEMENT: SkillPulse

## 1. Context & Motivation

In today's fast-paced software engineering ecosystem, the demand for technical proficiencies shifts rapidly. Technologies once considered mandatory (such as jQuery or legacy Hadoop) have ceded hiring volume to modern distributed frameworks, cloud-native container orchestration, and Generative AI application development.

Despite this dynamic reality, university students and engineering graduates commonly make career learning choices based on:
1. Social media hype, marketing narratives, and anecdotal developer opinions.
2. Static, outdated academic curricula that trail production hiring by several years.
3. Commercial coaching centers promoting expensive courses without transparent hiring demand data.

This fundamental information asymmetry leads to poor graduate employability, misdirected learning efforts, and severe frustration for both employers and job seekers.

---

## 2. Core Problem Definitions

1. **Lack of Empirical Compensation Intelligence:** Students do not know which specific skills actually produce a salary premium versus which skills are merely entry-level baseline expectations.
2. **Absence of Temporal Momentum Tracking:** Students cannot distinguish between stable long-term technologies, emerging high-growth tools, and declining legacy frameworks.
3. **Ambiguity in Role Requirements:** Job descriptions often feature sprawling "wish-lists" of 20+ requirements. Students struggle to identify which skills are truly critical versus merely optional.
4. **Geographic Compensation Disparities:** Students in non-metro or Tier-2/3 regions lack data on regional cost-of-living adjustments and remote-work viability across major Indian employment hubs.
5. **No Structured Bridge to Employment:** Existing employment portals (LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed) focus on advertising vacancies rather than helping students measure their skill gap or plan a structured roadmap.

---

## 3. The SkillPulse Objective

The objective of **SkillPulse – The Stock Market of Skills** is to:
1. Ingest, parse, and clean large-scale unstructured job market postings using **Apache Spark RDDs**.
2. Treat technical skills as financial market assets, calculating a normalized **Demand Index**, **24-Month Momentum Slope**, and **Salary Premium**.
3. Develop a transparent, mathematically sound **Naive Bayes Salary Classifier** trained directly with distributed RDD transformations.
4. Provide students with an interactive **Skill Gap Analyzer** and **Career Readiness Score** grounded in empirical hiring frequencies.
5. Deliver a socially beneficial, accessible, and high-performance open-source platform to empower job seekers, college placement cells, and career counselors.
